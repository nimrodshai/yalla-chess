# Chess piece images

These pieces are based on the **mpchess** piece set by Maxime Chupin
(https://github.com/chupinmaxime), as published in the lichess source
(https://github.com/lichess-org/lila, `public/piece/mpchess`), licensed under
the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later
(https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html).

Changes made for Yalla Chess (November 2026): recolored to a gray and white
palette with a dark outline and a shaded right side, re-cropped so each piece
sits at a set height on the square, the rook redrawn, and the knight's muzzle
and jaw replaced with a shape from the **celtic** knight by Maurizio Monge
(https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art), used under the MIT license below.

These modified images are distributed under the GNU General Public License,
version 3 or later.
